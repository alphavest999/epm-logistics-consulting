EPM Logistics Consulting

We help organizations see where their logistics money goes and make practical changes that lower cost without cutting service.

What we do

Logistics cost review

Analysis of shipping methods, carriers and service levels to find savings that still meet customer expectations.

Inventory and supplier strategy

Reorder rules, stock levels and supplier choices based on total cost, lead time and reliability.

Operations improvement

Clearer processes and shared information across orders, inventory and shipping.

Our approach

  1. Measure first. Start with the data on what is shipped, stored and returned
  2. Focus on the biggest levers. Address the few changes that matter most before the many small ones
  3. Make it visible. Simple shared reporting so teams catch problems early
  4. Review and adjust. Revisit results regularly and update the rules as the business grows
